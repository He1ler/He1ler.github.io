Throne Siege: RTS Strategy

Tactics Card GameLead Unity developer, gameplay + UI + release buildsTeam of 10Android, iOS, Web

A full mobile strategy product with its own meta game. Every round the player drafts cards to deploy units, buildings and buffs on a tile grid, then a deterministic auto-battle plays out against an AI opponent that drafts and picks boosters like a player. Twelve arenas, each with its own faction, units and card pool, are wrapped in a progression loop of card upgrades, chests, boosters, cups and a shop, all drawn in Spine skeletal animation.

Features & tech
  • Stack: Unity 6 (URP), Zenject DI, UniTask, DOTween, Spine; LevelPlay ads, IAP, Firebase Remote Config, AppMetrica, AppsFlyer
  • Mechanics: card drafting with drag-and-place and a placement ghost, per-phase state machines (building / battle / booster / shop), auto-battle with up to 7 units per cell, enemy AI that drafts cards and picks boosters
  • Content: 12 arenas with separate factions, units, buildings and buffs; card upgrades, chests and boosters
  • Milestones: a responsive battle simulation and UI on mobile, balance configs kept in sync with the GDD, an in-house analytics dashboard and event system, Firebase dependency resolution on Android, production builds shipped through a Unity 6 upgrade
Unity C# Zenject UniTask Spine DOTween
